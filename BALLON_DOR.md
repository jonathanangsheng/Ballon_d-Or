# Ballon d'Or — what the voters are asked to judge

Organised by *France Football*, co-run with UEFA since the 2024 edition. This repo covers the
men's award only.

## Official criteria (current, since the 2022 reform)

| # | Criterion | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Individual performances and decisive character** | goals, assists, key contributions, impact in decisive matches and moments |
| 2 | **Team performances and achievements** | trophies and results of the player's club and national team |
| 3 | **Class and fair play** | talent, sporting behaviour, conduct on and off the pitch |

Listed in order of priority; individual performance weighs most.

## What changed in 2022

| | Before 2022 | Since 2022 |
|---|---|---|
| Period judged | calendar year (Jan–Dec) | football season (Aug–Jul) |
| Career / longevity | a criterion | **removed** — only the season counts |

## How the vote works

- A shortlist of 30 nominees is drawn up by *France Football*'s editorial panel (with UEFA since 2024).
- One journalist per country votes — since 2024, from the top 100 nations in the FIFA ranking.
- Each voter ranks 10 players; points are 15, 12, 10, 8, 7, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 from 1st to 10th.
- Highest total wins.

_Compiled from general knowledge as of 2025; check the current edition's rules on France Football's site._
